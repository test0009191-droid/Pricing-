LPO Pricing

Keep each customer's prices and get them instantly for any LPO.
How does this work?
  1. Type the customer's account number and tap Open.
  2. Tab Add prices: paste the codes and prices copied from the customer's invoice. Say if the account has a discount (and how many %), and whether the prices are before or after it.
  3. Tab Get prices: paste the codes from the LPO and tap Show prices.
  4. Red rows are codes not saved yet: type the name and price, then tap Save.
  5. Tap Connect Excel file once so everything is saved to Excel automatically.
